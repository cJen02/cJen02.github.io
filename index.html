About

I work where mechanics, electronics and software meet. I'm a PhD student at IIT Gandhinagar, advised by Prof. Vineet Vashista in the Human-Centered Robotics Lab. My research uses physics-based musculoskeletal simulation to understand human movement and to design and control assistive robots. Beyond research, I enjoy robot modelling and control, simulators (MuJoCo, MyoSuite, CoppeliaSim, Gazebo), and deploying learned models on embedded hardware like the NVIDIA Jetson.
